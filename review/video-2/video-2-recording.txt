PHONE RECORDING TIPS
- Quiet room with soft things around (bed, curtains, closet of clothes). No fan or aircon blowing on the phone.
- Phone 15-20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side so "p" sounds don't pop.
- Use the voice recorder app, not video. Hold still; don't tap the phone.
- Smile while you talk: it sounds friendlier.
- Do 2-3 full takes. Keep the best one; small mistakes are fine, just re-read that line.
- Leave 1 second of silence at the start and end.
